# 🧐 About Saad

Hi there! Saad is a Master's (M.A.Sc.) student in **Electrical and Computer Engineering (Applied AI) at the [University of Ottawa](https://www.uottawa.ca/en)**, Canada, working under the supervision of [Prof. Abdulmotaleb El Saddik](https://www.site.uottawa.ca/~elsaddik/). His thesis, *OmniTutor*, explores a USD-based architecture for embodied conversational agents in simulated learning environments. Previously, Saad worked as a Graduate Research Assistant at the **Metaverse Center, [MBZUAI](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/)**, and as a Software Engineer - AI at **EPAM Systems (client: HUMAIN)**, Dubai.

**Research Interests**: Saad is interested in building AI systems that perceive, reason, and interact naturally with people and their environments. Specifically:
- Medical Image Analysis: AI methods for accurate diagnosis and clinically meaningful image analysis
- Human Action Recognition: detecting and classifying human activities for surveillance, sports, and healthcare
- Immersive Human-Computer Interaction: AI-driven interaction techniques for AR/VR/XR and metaverse environments
- Agentic AI: agents that can perceive, reason, plan, and autonomously interact with operating systems and applications

🤔 Saad is currently working on **OmniTutor** — a USD-based architecture for embodied conversational agents — and on Agentic Operating Systems for AI PCs at Dell Technologies. Please feel free to contact Saad at <span style="font-family:'Courier New', monospace;">msaad104 at uottawa dot ca</span> for collaboration.
